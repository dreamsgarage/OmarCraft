# OmarCraft 0.5.8 plan

**Status: approved 2026-11-09; built on `v0.5.8-dev` with 0.5.7 merged in, including the fix for the
marketplace review of 0.5.6 (#14528); released 2026-11-11 (the author: "OK for release"). 0.5.7 was not
released on its own: the author chose to submit 0.5.8 only (one review instead of two).** The design is Round 7 on the design canvas
(https://claude.ai/artifact/JCcaokq9mHv7Ju6FVoK74D), with the footer below added to it before the build.

## Why

1. **OmarCraft stopped reading ArtCraft's app list.** On 2026-11-06/07 ArtCraft changed the format of
   `storytold/craft-launcher` `crates/core/src/catalog.rs`: one-line `app("id", "Name", "Tagline", [[…]],
   "owner/repo")` records, no `AppGroup`, and a `CraftApp { kind: AppKind::Community, …, ..app(…) }` form for a
   community app. `parse_upstream_catalog` reads **0 apps** from it (checked live 2026-11-09), so the panel
   silently falls back to its built-in 13. No app ArtCraft adds or drops reaches users until this is fixed.
2. **ArtCraft lists 17 apps; OmarCraft shows 13.** New since 10-14: TextureCraft, MaterialCraft and Craft
   Anything (ArtCraft), and SolidCraft (Tangency Labs, a community app in ArtCraft's list).
3. **The panel does not scale to 20 tools.** It is sorted by status (UPDATES, INSTALLED, AVAILABLE, three
   tiles per line) and shows versions that mean nothing to users.

## Decisions (the author, 2026-11-09)

- **Logo:** the rocket, in the hero and on the bar (unchanged).
- **Three categories**, shown in this order since 2026-11-10 (the author): Office, Creative, 3D Art. MailCraft,
  a community app ArtCraft added on 2026-11-10 (no package yet), is Office.
  - **Creative:** PhotoCraft, VectorCraft, LightCraft, DesignCraft, FigCraft, FilmCraft, EffectCraft, SoundCraft
  - **3D Art:** CADCraft, SolidCraft, TextureCraft, MaterialCraft
  - **Office:** WordCraft, GridCraft, DeckCraft, PdfCraft, Typesetter, Memospot, Planify (Notes merged in)
- **Craft Anything:** included as a footer of its own, below the categories.
- **Collapsing:** option B, the accordion. Each category is one header line; Enter opens or closes it.
- **Look:** Round 6. No version numbers in the panel: an app has an update or it does not. After an update,
  one line names what was updated. Clean, without unnecessary information.
- **Follow the leader** (0.5.7): ArtCraft's list decides which Craft apps exist; OmarCraft follows it.

## Releases before their package (2026-11-10)

VectorCraft 0.23.0 offered its own update while OmarCraft showed none: ArtCraft released it on 2026-11-09 at
12:10 UTC, the AUR packaged it on 2026-11-10 at 03:22, and OmarCraft's last check had run at 03:14 (a check
then showed the badge). Two delays add up: ArtCraft to the AUR (hours, the packager's), and the AUR to
OmarCraft's next check (up to six hours, or `r`). **The author's decision: keep the package.** OmarCraft
installs only packages, never ArtCraft's files directly.

## Design

### Panel
1. **Hero** unchanged: rocket, OmarCraft, `16 OF 20 INSTALLED · 2 UPDATES`, refresh button.
2. **Summary line** (new), under the hero, after a successful update job (`update` or `system-update`, exit
   0): `✓ Updated PhotoCraft and WordCraft` (names only; "and N more" past three). It goes on the next key,
   after 8 seconds, or when the panel closes. It is read from `last-job.json`, like the failed-job flag
   today, and marked seen the same way.
3. **UPDATES strip** when updates are pending: `UPDATES · N`, the app names on one line, *Update all · u*.
   The tiles no longer move to the top. They stay in their category with an **Update** badge, so no app
   shows twice.
4. **Categories**, as accordions: header = chevron, name, `N of M`, an *Update* dot, *New*, and the category's
   key while something in it is not installed. Open: the category's tiles (installed: tinted; not installed:
   outlined and dimmed), then *Install N · key* if any are missing. **Open or closed is remembered** in the
   widget's settings (`openCategories`, like `layout`). First run: all closed. A category that gains an app
   the user has not seen opens itself once.
5. **Footer: Craft Anything.** One line below the last category, after a separator, never collapsed: its
   glyph, its name, and *Install*, *Update* or *New* where they apply. It has no category key; Enter on it
   installs, launches or updates it. It is drawn on Round 7 before the build.
6. **Badges** read *Update* and *New*, never a version. Everywhere in the panel, versions go: tile badges,
   rows, confirm strips ("Update WordCraft · a terminal will open and ask for your password"), subtexts
   ("New · install" without the version). Versions stay in the command line (`omarcraft list`, `check`) and
   for people who want them. The six-hourly notification lists names only, to match (the author, 2026-11-09).
7. **Rows layout** (`t`) stays: inside each open category the apps show as rows instead of tiles, without
   versions (the author, 2026-11-09).
8. **Keys:** arrows move through open categories, their headers and the footer; Enter opens or closes a header,
   and on an app launches, installs or updates it; `c` Creative, `3` 3D Art, `o` Office install what is
   missing there; `u` Update all; `x` remove; `r` refresh; `t` layout; Esc closes. `n` goes.

### Catalog and categories (`bin/omarcraft`)
1. **Parser** for the new format: one-line and multi-line `app(…)` records, and the `CraftApp {…, ..app(…)}`
   form, taking id, name, tagline, the three colours and `kind` (`suite` or `community`, in the group column).
   0.5.5's bounds stay: 256 KB download, field caps, at most 64 apps, no control characters. The old
   multi-line format keeps parsing too, so a revert upstream does no harm.
2. **A parse of 0 apps from a non-empty download is a failure**, not a silent fallback. The built-in list is
   used as now, `refresh` records `catalog-parse-failed`, and `omarcraft check` and `list` print one line about
   it. The panel stays clean. That is the early warning for the next format change.
3. **Built-in list** updated to ArtCraft's 17, in ArtCraft's order, with their colours.
4. **Categories** replace the groups: `GROUP_OF` takes `creative` (default), `3d` and `office`, plus `solo`
   for Craft Anything. `install-group` accepts `all`, `creative`, `3d`, `office`. **`notes` stays as an alias
   of `office`**, so an opted-in Omarchy menu entry written by an earlier version keeps working. The menu
   section (`setup-menu`) gets *Install 3D Art* and loses *Install Notes*.
5. **Glyphs** (Nerd Font MDI) for the four new apps, checked by rendering as in `docs/v1-design.md`.
6. **Cache format 6**: the first read after the update fetches the list at once, so the new apps appear
   without waiting six hours. Apps that were seen before are not flagged *New* again; the four new ones are.

### Not changing
- Sources: the AUR first, with the `aur/` prefix where a repo ships the name (0.5.7, `FOLLOW_APP_REPOS=0`).
- Typesetter and Planify from the Arch repos with Omarchy's update. Memospot from the AUR.
- Install, update and remove jobs, the compile guard, network bounds.
